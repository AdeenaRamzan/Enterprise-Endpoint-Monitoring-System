COMPANY MONITORING POLICY (sample text -- replace with your real policy)

This computer is monitored by the IT department as follows:
- A screenshot is captured every 10 seconds while the agent is running.
- Active/idle status is tracked based on keyboard and mouse activity.
- IT staff may start a remote screen-viewing or remote-control session
  for support purposes. You will always see a visible indicator in the
  system tray when a remote session is active.
- Every time an IT staff member or manager views your screenshots or
  starts a remote session, that access is logged and auditable.

Monitoring data is used only for the purposes described above. Contact
IT via the tray icon menu with any questions.
